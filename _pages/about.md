      <h1>Mathematics &amp; Geometry</h1>

      <p class="emma-hero__intro">
        I am María Emma Eyrea Irazú, a mathematician and researcher
        at the National University of La Plata (UNLP), Argentina.
        I hold a Bachelor's degree and a Ph.D. in Mathematics from UNLP.
      </p>

      <p>
        My research lies in geometric mechanics and mathematical physics,
        with particular interest in contact and symplectic mechanics,
        forced and time-dependent systems, hybrid mechanical systems,
        and Hamilton–Jacobi theory, with applications to robotics
        and dynamical systems.
      </p>

      <p>
        I am also interested in symmetry and reduction, Lie group actions,
        momentum maps, and geometric structures arising in mechanical systems.
      </p>

      <p>
        Alongside my research, I teach mathematics at the Faculty of Exact
        Sciences at UNLP and participate in academic and educational activities.
      </p>
