I am María Emma Eyrea Irazú, a mathematician and researcher at the National University of La Plata (UNLP), Argentina. I hold a Bachelor's degree and a Ph.D. in Mathematics from UNLP.

My research lies in **geometric mechanics and mathematical physics**, with a particular interest in the geometric and dynamical aspects of mechanical systems. My research includes **contact and symplectic mechanics, forced and time-dependent systems, hybrid mechanical systems, and Hamilton–Jacobi theory**, with applications to robotics and dynamical systems.

I am also interested in **symmetry and reduction, Lie group actions, momentum maps, and geometric structures arising in mechanical systems**.

Alongside my research, I am involved in teaching mathematics at the Faculty of Exact Sciences at UNLP, where I teach undergraduate courses and participate in academic and educational activities.
