My research is in **geometric mechanics and mathematical physics**, with a focus on the geometric structures underlying mechanical and dynamical systems.

### Geometric Mechanics

I study geometric formulations of classical mechanics, including **symplectic, contact, and cosymplectic structures**, as well as their relationships with dynamical systems.

### Contact and Symplectic Mechanics

A central part of my research concerns **contact and symplectic mechanics**, including contact Hamiltonian systems, contact reduction, and geometric formulations of dissipative and non-conservative dynamics.

### Forced and Time-dependent Systems

I am interested in the geometric treatment of **forced and time-dependent mechanical systems**, with particular emphasis on reduction, Hamilton–Jacobi theory, and the underlying geometric structures.

### Hybrid Mechanical Systems

I study **hybrid mechanical systems**, combining continuous dynamics with discrete events such as impacts and transitions. These models have applications to **robotics and bipedal locomotion**.

### Hamilton–Jacobi Theory

Another research direction concerns **Hamilton–Jacobi theory** in geometric mechanics, including applications to nonholonomic, forced, contact, and hybrid systems.

### Symmetry and Reduction

I work on **symmetries, momentum maps, and reduction procedures** for mechanical systems, including geometric reduction in symplectic, contact, and polysymplectic settings.

### Current Interests

My current interests include the interaction between **geometric mechanics, contact geometry, dynamical systems, and control**, as well as the development of geometric and numerical methods for mechanical systems.
