MATHEMATICS · GEOMETRY

Emma Eyrea

Researcher in geometric mechanics
and mathematical physics

I am a mathematician and researcher at the National University of La Plata (UNLP), Argentina. I hold a Bachelor's degree and a Ph.D. in Mathematics from UNLP.

My research focuses on geometric mechanics and mathematical physics, including contact and symplectic mechanics, forced and time-dependent systems, hybrid mechanical systems, and Hamilton–Jacobi theory.

WHAT I WORK ON

Research

Geometric ideas and mathematical structures for understanding mechanical and dynamical systems.

01

Geometric mechanics

Geometric formulations of classical mechanics, including symplectic, contact and cosymplectic structures.

02

Contact and symplectic systems

Contact Hamiltonian systems, contact reduction, and geometric descriptions of dissipative dynamics.

03

Forced and time-dependent systems

Geometric approaches to forced and time-dependent mechanical systems, reduction and Hamilton–Jacobi theory.

04

Hybrid mechanical systems

Systems combining continuous dynamics with discrete events such as impacts and transitions, with applications to robotics.

05

Symmetry and reduction

Symmetries, momentum maps and reduction procedures in geometric mechanics.

06

Hamilton–Jacobi theory

Hamilton–Jacobi methods for nonholonomic, forced, contact and hybrid systems.
